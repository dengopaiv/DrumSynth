# Credits

## DrumSynth

- **DrumSynth 2.0** (1998–2000) is by **Paul Kellett** (mda, http://www.mda-vst.com). This edition recreates his program, his sound engine and his help text, and ships the preset library that came with DrumSynth 2.0.
- **The accessible edition** (4.0) is developed at https://github.com/dengopaiv/DrumSynth.

## Kits

- **Dark** is made by the artist **Darkside**. His music can be found at https://soundcloud.com/dRxId.
- The other kits come from the library shipped with DrumSynth 2.0 (`bin/library.zip`) and the later `drumsynths/` collection. Their individual authors aren't recorded in this repository; if you know who made a kit, please tell us, so they can be credited here.

## Third-party components

The program uses these, unmodified, as separate DLLs. Their licences are in `licenses/` in the download, listed in `licenses/THIRD-PARTY.txt`:
- **wxWidgets**, and the image libraries it uses (libjpeg-turbo, libpng, libtiff, libwebp, liblzma, zlib, PCRE2);
- **Tolk** by Davy Kager, and **NVDA's controller client** (NV Access), for speaking messages through screen readers;
- the **Microsoft Visual C++ runtime**.
